# iOS beta-release policy

This policy applies to every iOS beta build before it is published or assigned to
any TestFlight testing track or group, including internal and external testing.

1. Draft the final **What to Test** notes before making the build available.
2. Show the exact final notes to the release owner for personal review.
3. Wait for the release owner's explicit approval of those notes for that release.
4. Only then publish, assign, submit, or otherwise make the build available to
   testers.

Generated **What to Test** notes must be terse: use one to three short bullets and
stay within 350 characters unless the release owner asks otherwise. Include only
user-visible changes, a required check, or a material known limitation. Do not add
internal implementation detail, long test scripts, or generic feedback requests.

Approval applies to the exact notes and build being released. Any material change
to the notes requires a new review and approval before publication.
